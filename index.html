The Creative Thesis LENS▶
Internal thesis (never post this literally)
"What happens when a curious 48-year-old decides life isn't supposed to get smaller?"
BEFORE POSTING ANYTHING, ASK:
1. Would a stranger stop?
2. Does it add a NEW perception?
3. Is there an open loop or curiosity?
4. Is it distinctly ME?
5. Would someone send this to a friend?
6. Does NOT posting improve the account?
7. What audience behavior am I creating: stop, watch, reply, save, share, follow?

If it's only worth a "like" — skip it.
CONTENT CLASSIFICATION
POST NOW — strong enough for grid today
STORY — 1-3 frames, intimacy + texture
SAVE FOR SERIES — bank for carousel/Reel
REEL INGREDIENT — capture video, edit later
SKIP — live it, don't document it
EDITORIAL FRANCHISES
DC, NOT THE POSTCARD — what I noticed while living here
BEGINNER AT 48 — learning, awkwardness, reinvention
VEGETARIAN IN THE WILD — food discoveries with an opinion
ONE GOOD QUESTION — people + ideas, 10-20 sec
ELSEWHERE — travel as observation, not tourism
AI IN REAL LIFE — judgment, not enthusiasm. ~15-20% of content
AI FRANCHISES (overlay on other pillars)
AI FIELD NOTES — short, skeptical observations from real use
I LET AI... — real experiments with actual outcomes
HUMAN VS AI — bartender vs AI, local vs AI, instructor vs AI
AI MADE ME NOTICE... — AI deepens observation of city/travel

Rule: AI overlays existing pillars. Helsinki = Elsewhere + AI. Restaurant = Vegetarian + Human vs AI. Midnight coding = Building + Beginner at 48. Don't force it. Find it only when it's real.

Never: reveal employer, client info, Holicow details, internal code. Abstract the lesson.
CAPTION RULE
Add a second layer. Don't narrate the image. Short, specific, witty, understated. The image shows what happened — the caption shows how you think.
