Option A — Tower of London + Tower Bridge
Walk east from hotel (20 min). Tower of London £37, book online, arrive 9am opening. Crown Jewels, White Tower, Beefeater tours. 2 hrs. Then walk back via Tower Bridge. Best for: history, iconic experience.

Tube to Holborn (20 min). FREE. Rosetta Stone, Egyptian mummies, Elgin Marbles. 90 min max — don't try to do it all. Walk through Covent Garden after. Best for: culture, rain day.

Tube to Notting Hill Gate (20 min). Pastel houses, market stalls, Golborne Road for local feel. Coffee + walk. Best for: neighborhood vibes, photography.
